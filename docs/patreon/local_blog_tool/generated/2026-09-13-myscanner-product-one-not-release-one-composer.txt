TITLE: Why MyScanner Is Product 1, Not Release 1
VISIBILITY: Public

This draft is written as a public-facing Patreon update, with enough context for someone following the project without needing to track every internal implementation detail.

Today’s focus is why myscanner is product 1, not release 1, which sits at the intersection of the project’s public story and the practical work needed to keep the platform moving.

MyScanner has been designated as BiblionOCR's first standalone productization track, with an explicit boundary between strategic priority and release readiness.

A priority, not a premature promise:

MyScanner is now the first active standalone productization track. That means its boundaries, dependencies, packaging needs, hardware behavior, licensing, and support model receive focused attention first.

It does not mean a supported binary is ready today. The project documentation now distinguishes Product 1 status from technical-preview and supported-release readiness so an internal priority cannot be mistaken for a public promise.

Two related tracks:

BiblionOCR remains an open-source ecosystem. Separately productized module binaries may help fund continued development, beginning with scanner technology, but that commercial boundary must be designed and reviewed rather than inferred after packaging.

Current blockers remain explicit: dependency and repository coupling, standalone build manifests, scanner-backend tests, hardware acceptance, and licensing review all have to be resolved before release.
